# Horizontal swipe V1.1

Exactly three fingers detected as extended (including thumb) arm horizontal swipe with either hand. No particular combination or handedness is required. Raw mirrored palm centroid displacement, average speed and horizontal dominance are combined with a minimum fuzzy T-norm. A stationary or mainly vertical motion does not trigger. One swipe is emitted until two non-three-finger samples release the latch. During the pose and release transition, tap and vertical gesture recognition are suppressed; a held pinch is not converted into a new tap on release. A brief tracking loss does not rearm a completed swipe. Existing vertical touch trajectories and open-hand pointer behavior otherwise remain unchanged. The existing right-hand one/two-finger gestures continue to scroll content down/up respectively, and the former left-hand static horizontal commands are removed. No new dependencies or camera streams. Thresholds are initial engineering choices; all ten physical finger combinations and both hands need real-device verification. Unit tests exercise the motion engine, not landmark classification or Android gesture injection.
