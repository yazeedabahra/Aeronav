# Yvora 1.8-dev: personal fuzzy learning and two-contact zoom

Includes all prior stacked branches #10–#12. The new supervised trainer is deliberately limited to variations of the horizontal swipe pose rather than arbitrary gesture recognition. A safe local studio collects at least ten accepted frames over 2–12 seconds, requiring tracking quality, separated thumb, and minimum edge/extension/together memberships. Inconsistent examples are rejected (at least 85% similarity to mean >=.80); users review and explicitly save an averaged three-value template per hand. Fuzzy Jaccard = sum(min) / sum(max); the learned rule uses min(similarity, tracking quality), then max with the original assisted rule. Existing two-frame and motion requirements remain, assisted mode must be enabled, and templates can be forgotten. No raw image, landmarks, identity data, or remote training requests are saved/sent.

Experimental zoom-only mode is opt-in and requires dual-hand mode plus engine restart. In this mode individual hand taps, drags and swipes are suppressed. Arm with two open palms, close both thumb/index pinches, move hands apart or closer. Fuzzy inference combines absolute and relative distance change with tracking/identity quality; alpha .55, bounded time, fresh frames and minimum separation; one zoom per cycle, rearm with open palms. Two simultaneous Android touch strokes are dispatched at the display center for 320ms; callback/busy guard excludes single touch and AI navigation while zoom is executing. This is a discrete two-contact gesture, not continuous multitouch or rotation; apps must support pinch zoom. A dispatched stroke may finish after stopping tracking. No promise of effect on every app. Real-device validation needed. No new model or camera stream. AI routing, arbitrary custom gesture trajectories, continuous zoom, rotation and general two-finger drag remain future work.
